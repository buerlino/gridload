GridLoad tells you at a glance whether now is a good time to run household appliances like the washing machine, dishwasher or EV charger. It reads your utility's dynamic electricity price and shows one colour:

<ul>
<li><b>Green, good time:</b> electricity is cheap, run your appliances now.</li>
<li><b>Orange, fair time:</b> average price, only run what you need.</li>
<li><b>Red, bad time:</b> electricity is expensive, wait if you can.</li>
</ul>

Each 15-minute price is compared with the rest of the day. Cheap times are when the grid has the most to spare, for example at midday when solar power peaks. Shifting load to those times lowers your bill and helps the grid.

<b>Regions:</b> the price comes from your local utility, so GridLoad only works where a utility publishes a dynamic tariff. For now that is Central Switzerland (CKW). More regions are planned.

<b>Privacy:</b> no account, no ads, no tracking. The only permission is internet access, used to load the day's prices from the utility.

Planned: a peak load mode that helps keep your household's monthly peak low.
