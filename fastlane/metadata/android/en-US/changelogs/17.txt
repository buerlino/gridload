- Price curve: the next 24 hours in their colours, with how long green lasts or when it comes.
- Limit (Settings → Mode): a switch per part of the limit, and where the limit comes from.
- Base load: what your house always draws, below the history.
- Share: tell the household when a ¼ hour heads for the limit.
- "Sets a new peak" only above the month's highest.
- Vibrate at the limit works with notification vibration off; tap a choice to try it.
