- Price curve: the next 24 hours as bars in their colours, with how long green lasts or when it comes (Settings → Region can hide it).
- Limit (Settings → Mode): one switch per part of the limit, with its value and where the limit comes from.
- Share: tell the household when a ¼ hour heads for the limit.
- "Sets a new peak" only above the month's highest, else "Reaches the limit".
- Vibrate at the limit works with notification vibration off; tap a choice to try it.
