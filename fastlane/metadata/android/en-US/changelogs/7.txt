- Load data is now a week of hourly values: import 7 daily Excel exports from the CKW customer portal (the current month, or the same month last year). Year and month exports are no longer used.
- Peak load mode takes each hour's usual draw from them, like a water heater at night, and asks for the import right on its main screen when the month's week is missing
- Spot price mode can use the data too: what your home usually draws at this hour and what that costs
